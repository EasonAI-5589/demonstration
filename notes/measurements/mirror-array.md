## Observed

- Source GIF is 640×360, 17 frames at 330 ms each, 5.61 seconds.
- A perspective mirror wall sits in a light room. The 21×9 tile layout is consistent with the article’s 189-mirror description.
- Image-space panel corners used by the reconstruction: (111,21), (491,76), (491,269), (111,323), in source pixels.
- Bright window reflections occupy the left side, a dark fixture sits near the center, and the visitor is at the right. The motion starts in the left mirror columns and spreads across the array.

## Reconstruction and limits

- Native Canvas vectors reconstruct the room, tiled panel, reflected light fields and visitor silhouette. The real person and photographic texture are not replicated pixel for pixel.
- Hinge angles, observer motion and reflection field are inferred from the recording. No depth map, original program or sensor data was obtained.
- The optional pointer input steers the hinge front; when absent, a deterministic recorded-style sweep plays. Reduced motion freezes that sweep and visitor at a representative phase.
- Clip endpoints are not claimed to form a seamless physical motion cycle.

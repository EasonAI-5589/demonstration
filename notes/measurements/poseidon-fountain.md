## Reconstruction and limits

- Native Canvas particles are calculated analytically from elapsed time and fixed hashes; no stateful simulation is retained between frames.
- The rendering preserves the sparse scale and negative space of the source rather than adding a dashboard or oversized glow.
- The GIF contains no audio. No audio response, calibrated fluid model, or original musical data is claimed. Emitter count, droplet trajectories and the pulse envelope are inferred from visible pixels.
- Reduced motion freezes the particle phase at a representative source-like moment. Clip endpoints may cut when looping, matching the limited source recording rather than inventing a measured seamless cycle.
